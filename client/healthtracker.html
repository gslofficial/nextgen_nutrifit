    THE SCIENCE

    How the numbers are calculated

    Every formula and limit below comes from a published scientific or public-health source.

    01 · ENERGY

    Mifflin-St Jeor + activity factor

    BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age + 5 (men) or −161 (women). Multiplied by a physical-activity level (PAL) from 1.2 to 1.9, in line with FAO/WHO/UNU energy-requirement reports. Fat loss uses a moderate deficit (up to 500 kcal, max 20%); lean gain adds 300 kcal. Floors of 1,500 kcal (men) / 1,200 kcal (women) are applied.

    02 · BODY SIZE

    WHO BMI categories

    Under 18.5 underweight, 18.5-24.9 healthy, 25-29.9 overweight, 30+ obese. An optional Asian-population set (23 and 27.5) follows the WHO Expert Consultation (Lancet, 2004). BMI is a screening tool and does not measure body fat or muscle.

    03 · MACROS

    IOM AMDR + ISSN protein

    Protein, carbohydrate and fat are kept within the Acceptable Macronutrient Distribution Ranges (protein 10-35%, carbohydrate 45-65%, fat 20-35% of energy). Protein per kg ranges from 0.8 g (RDA) to 2.0 g (ISSN position stand for exercising adults), based on your activity.

    04 · FIBER & WATER

    IOM fiber, EFSA water

    Fiber: 14 g per 1,000 kcal (IOM Dietary Reference Intakes). Water: 2.5 L/day for men and 2.0 L/day for women as total water from drinks and food (EFSA adequate intake). Needs rise in heat and with heavy exercise.

    05 · LIMITS

    WHO healthy-diet limits

    Free sugars under 10% of energy (under 5% gives extra benefit), saturated fat under 10% of energy, and sodium under 2 g/day (about 5 g salt).

    06 · REFERENCES

    Read the originals
